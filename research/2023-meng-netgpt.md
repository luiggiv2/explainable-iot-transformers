- **Models / Methods**: First generative pre-trained transformer (GPT-style, decoder) targeted at network traffic. Introduces "multi-pattern network traffic modeling" to build unified text inputs from header fields and payload segments; uses header-field shuffling and packet segmentation as pre-training augmentations, plus task prompts/labels for downstream adaptation. Supports both traffic *understanding* (classification) and traffic *generation*.

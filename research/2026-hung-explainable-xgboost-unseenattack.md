- **Dataset(s)**: CICIoT2023 (46,686,579 total samples, 46 numerical flow-based features; 2,000,000-sample training subset used for fair-budget comparison, ~97.65% attack / ~2.35% benign)
- **Models / Methods**: Binary (malicious vs. benign) classification benchmark of seven models under identical training budgets — optimized XGBoost (primary), default XGBoost, Random Forest, LightGBM, CatBoost, Logistic Regression, simple MLP; XGBoost's native SHAP-compatible `pred_contribs` used for feature attribution, plus a fixed-false-alarm-rate (FAR) calibration protocol and repeated (5-seed, 45-run) unseen-attack-family evaluation
- **Key metrics reported**: Closed-world, fair-budget — Random Forest macro-F1 0.9713 (MCC 0.9659, FAR 0.0499); optimized XGBoost macro-F1 0.9566 (MCC 0.9160); default XGBoost FAR as low as 0.0018. Unseen-attack-family, fair-budget — RF macro-F1 0.8433 (FNR 0.0712); optimized XGBoost macro-F1 0.8194 (FAR 0.0086), repeated-eval macro-F1 0.8196 ± 0.1812 (FAR 0.0090 ± 0.0026). Full-scale optimized XGBoost — closed-world macro-F1 0.9560, balanced accuracy 0.9969, FAR 0.0017; unseen-family macro-F1 0.8300, MCC 0.7344, FAR 0.0021. Fixed-FAR (≈0.01) calibration — RF macro-F1 0.8754 ± 0.0962 (attack recall 0.80); optimized XGBoost macro-F1 0.8323 ± 0.1603 (attack recall 0.724)
- **XAI method (if any)**: XGBoost native SHAP-compatible feature-contribution analysis (margin-space `pred_contribs`) — global feature ranking (top features: rst_count, IAT, urg_count, Tot_size, Number) plus local TP/FP/FN/TN case studies; reconstruction validated to ≤1.71×10⁻⁷ error
- **Relevance to our RQs**: One of the most directly citable **CICIoT2023 RF-vs-XGBoost head-to-head results with exact macro-F1/MCC/FAR numbers** at both closed-world and unseen-attack-family (out-of-distribution) evaluation — an excellent anchor for RQ1 (classical baselines) and uniquely relevant to RQ4 (robustness), since its unseen-family and fixed-FAR calibration protocols are conceptually adjacent to our planned explanation-robustness-under-perturbation analysis (though at the model-generalization level rather than the explanation level). Its top-feature ranking (rst_count, IAT, urg_count) is a direct point of comparison for our RQ2 domain-knowledge cross-referencing.
- **Stated limitations**: Single-dataset evaluation (CICIoT2023 only); the "unseen attack family" protocol should be read as unseen-family/zero-day-*like*, not a full open-world zero-day benchmark; formulation is binary (malicious vs. benign) only, not multiclass attack attribution; FAR calibration was validation-based within the same benchmark environment, without modeling temporal/concept drift; relies on XGBoost's native `pred_contribs` rather than a fully harmonized multi-backend explanation comparison (e.g., no SHAP KernelExplainer or Captum cross-check); authors flag the need for analyst-facing explanation workflows and human-subject evaluation as future work.
- **Quotable finding (paraphrased)**: Under an identical training budget on CICIoT2023, Random Forest achieves the highest closed-world macro-F1 (0.9713) and recall, but optimized XGBoost achieves a markedly lower false-alarm rate — a precision/recall trade-off that persists (and worsens for both models) under unseen-attack-family evaluation, where macro-F1 drops roughly 10-15 points from the closed-world figure, showing that strong in-distribution CICIoT2023 performance does not reliably predict generalization to attack families absent from training.

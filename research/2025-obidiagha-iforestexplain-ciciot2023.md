However, it targets only the binary DoS/DDoS anomaly-detection subset rather than the full multi-class attack taxonomy we plan to use, and does not test explanation robustness under perturbation (**RQ4**) or per-attack-category bias across the dataset's other categories (**RQ3**) — both open angles for our contribution.

- **Quotable finding (paraphrased)**: A two-stage Isolation Forest pipeline — SMOTE oversampling followed by correlation-based feature selection — reaches near-perfect DoS/DDoS anomaly detection on CICIoT2023 (F1 up to 0.94, AUC above 0.99), with SHAP and LIME confirming which flow features drive the anomaly scores, demonstrating that CICIoT2023 supports high explanatory fidelity at least for its DoS/DDoS subset, though the study leaves open whether these explanations generalize to the dataset's other attack categories (Mirai, reconnaissance, spoofing, web-based, brute force) or remain stable under input perturbation.

- **Dataset(s)**: Nine public benchmark tabular datasets, confirmed via the paper's full text (Section 4.1, cross-checked against a secondary paper citing the same table): Bank (45,211 rows, 16 feats; predict term-deposit subscription), Blood (748, 4; blood-donation return), California/Calhousing (20,640, 8; median house value), Car (1,728, 8; car safety evaluation), Credit-g (1,000, 20; credit approval), Diabetes (768, 8), Heart (918, 11; coronary artery disease), Income (48,842, 14; >$50K income), Jungle (44,819, 6; jungle chess outcome). Additionally, three healthcare prediction tasks (End of Life, Surgery, Likelihood of Hospitalization) were evaluated on a proprietary/de-identified health-claims dataset, not a public benchmark.
- **Models / Methods**: Systematically compares strategies for serializing tabular rows into natural-language text (plain "Text Template" listing "column is value" pairs, table-to-text generation models, and LLM-generated serializations) and then fine-tunes/uses large language models (zero-shot and few-shot, via in-context or parameter-efficient fine-tuning) as tabular classifiers.
- **Key metrics reported**: Authors report their serialization+LLM approach "outperforms prior deep-learning-based tabular classification methods" and is "competitive with strong traditional baselines like gradient-boosted trees," especially in low-data (few-shot) regimes; exact numeric accuracy/AUC tables not retrievable from fetched content — `[VERIFICAR]`.
- **XAI method (if any)**: None.
- **Relevance to our RQs**: Foundational to RQ1's core methodological premise — this is the paper that established and empirically validated the "serialize tabular row as a natural-language sentence, then fine-tune a language model" paradigm that our paper applies specifically to IoT flow features. It is not IoT/network-traffic-specific, so we cite it as the methodological origin of tabular-to-text serialization rather than as a direct competitor.
- **Stated limitations**: Text serialization does not scale well as the number of features grows (prompts can exceed model context length) — a directly relevant caveat for IoT flow records with many numeric fields, worth citing in our methodology/limitations discussion.
- **Quotable finding (paraphrased)**: Converting structured tabular rows into short natural-language sentences and feeding them to a pretrained language model — rather than engineering a separate tabular architecture — is a data-efficient way to reach classification performance competitive with gradient-boosted trees, particularly when labeled data is scarce.
